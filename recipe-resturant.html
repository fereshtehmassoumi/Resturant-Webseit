Spageti with special sauce

Ingredients
Value Material
500grSpageti
200grLame meat
300grTomato
100 grOnion
1 tspSalt and Peper

Step 1: Mix Tomato and cook it with hight tempreture , then add salt and peper. Boiling the 1 liter water in pot.

Step 2: This One-Pan Spaghetti with a Smoky Tomato Sauce is easy to make and done in just 30 minutes. The perfect dish for a busy weeknight dinner or for a relaxed weekend lunch.
